A CONNECTION YOU CAN UNDERSTAND

What’s behind your match?

A transparent score built from your skills, experience and the work you describe.

THE SKILLMATCH FORMULA
Text similarity × Certification × Experience

A weighted relevance score between 0 and 3.6 — not a hiring probability.

One engine. Both directions.

  1. Start with the work.

    Candidate skill tags, professional summary and project descriptions are combined. Jobs contribute the title, description and required skills.

  2. Compare the words.

    Text is tokenized, English stop words are removed, and each term contributes at most twice. TF-IDF and cosine similarity measure the text overlap.

  3. Apply the profile’s levels.

    The similarity is multiplied by the candidate’s certification and experience factors, then results are ranked from highest to lowest.

The same steps recommend jobs to a job seeker. Because one person’s multipliers are constant across their recommendations, they change the score but not the order of those jobs.

Certification multiplier

Basic × 1.0
Intermediate × 1.3
Advanced × 1.6
Expert × 2.0

Experience multiplier

Junior × 1.0
Mid × 1.2
Senior × 1.5
Lead × 1.8

What the score can tell you

It measures keyword relevance with the specified level weighting. It does not understand synonyms, verify credentials, or guarantee job suitability. Duplicate skills are prevented and repeated words have limited influence.

Supporting links let employers inspect your work. The links themselves do not contribute to your score, and SkillMatch does not verify their content. Matching skill tags highlight exact overlaps; other profile text can also contribute to similarity.
